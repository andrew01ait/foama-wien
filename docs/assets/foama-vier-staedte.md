## Gestaltungsbrief

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: production website hero illustration for the Austrian public transport app Foama, specifically its Cities & Features page. Create one extra-wide landscape image, approximately 2.4:1, ideally 2400 by 1000 pixels. Four Austrian cityscapes overlap and flow naturally into each other in one cohesive panorama, in the order Wien, Graz, Linz, Salzburg. No boxed panels. Recognisable landmarks: Vienna's Stephansdom and Prater Ferris wheel; Graz's Schlossberg Uhrturm and softly rounded Kunsthaus; Linz's glass Ars Electronica Center at the Danube and distant Pöstlingberg church; Salzburg's Hohensalzburg fortress and cathedral with a gentle Alpine silhouette. Use a polished contemporary editorial illustration, lightly dimensional architecture, clean shapes, refined detail, welcoming and energetic, no people. In the foreground, elegant rails, road ribbons and subtle mint motion lines weave through the city collage, carrying a red-and-white underground train, a green-and-white tram, a yellow-and-white tram and a red-and-white trolleybus. This is a decorative city collage, not a geographic route map. Palette: deep navy #071724 and #10283a, mint #73e0b7, warm ivory architecture, restrained teal and terracotta accents. Maintain good separation of the buildings and vehicles; avoid visual clutter. Integrate professional, crisp, generously sized readable sans-serif typography in clear sky space at the top: exact main title 'Foama', then the exact city line 'Wien – Graz – Linz – Salzburg'. These are the only words in the image. Keep all four city landmarks and every vehicle fully visible with generous edge margins. No additional logos, labels, warnings, UI elements, buttons, mockup frame, watermark or fine print. The image should feel like a coherent premium web illustration and remain recognisable as a compact mobile header.
